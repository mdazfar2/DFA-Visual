# 🎉 Welcome to DFA-Visual 👋  

**DFA-Visual** is an **interactive platform** to learn and simulate **Deterministic Finite Automata (DFA)** in real-time.  
Built especially for students of **Theory of Computation (TOC)**, this tool makes it easier to **visualize transitions, states, and regex-to-DFA conversions** with animations and an attractive dark-themed UI.  

---

👉 Visit https://dfavisual.vercel.app to explore!

The goal of **DFA-Visual** is to help learners understand **how DFAs work step by step** by simply entering:  

- ✍️ States & Input Alphabets  
- ⚙️ Transition Rules  
- 🚀 Regular Expressions  

and instantly **see the automaton come alive** with **animations and state transitions**.

---

<div>
  <h2><img src="https://github.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/blob/master/Emojis/Objects/Direct%20Hit.png?raw=true" width="35" height="35"> Benefits</h2>
</div>

- 🎓 **Learn By Doing** – Understand DFA by directly creating and testing it.  
- ⚡ **Regex → DFA Converter** – Instantly convert any regular expression into an animated DFA.  
- 🎥 **Live Animation** – Watch states change and transitions flow like a real machine.  
- 🌑 **Dark Themed UI** – Attractive, futuristic design to keep learners engaged.  
- 📖 **Educational Tool** – Perfect for students and teachers in TOC classes.  

---

## 🧩 Key Features  

- ✨ **Interactive DFA Builder** – Add states, alphabets, and transitions directly.  
- 🔄 **Regex to DFA** – Convert regular expressions into fully working DFA diagrams.  
- 🎬 **Real-Time Simulation** – Enter an input string and watch the DFA process step by step.  
- 🎨 **3D Animated Visualization** – Transitions & states are displayed with smooth animations.  
- 📊 **Clean, Minimal UI** – Designed with dark mode for better readability.
